Edits

Brush: drag to paint. Polygon: click the corners, double-click or Enter to close, Backspace removes a corner, Esc cancels.
Pathway: Brush = drag freehand, Polygon = click the bends then Enter / double-click. It snaps to an entrance at its start and the artifact at its end, then asks which artifact and entrance. Drawn as a route, with no floor under it.
Place: pick a crate colour (or Note) in the list, then click the map.
Remove mark: click a crate, note, entrance... to remove it; click its X to bring it back. A placed one is deleted outright.
Pan: right-drag (or Space + drag). Zoom: mouse wheel. [ ] brush size.
Erase trail removes recorded trail where you paint (floor and line). Erase line only takes the green pathway line off; the floor stays. Your recordings are never changed; edits are saved beside them and applied on every render.
